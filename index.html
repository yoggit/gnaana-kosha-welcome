Change a word, press Run, and the answer appears underneath. Getting it wrong costs nothing — that's how children find out that code does exactly what you say, not what you meant.

Conditions of use

The simple promises & the fine print

Plain-language terms for parents and guardians. Using Gnaana-Kosha means you're happy with these.

01 Who it's for & access

Gnaana-Kosha is a private learning hub for children in roughly grades 3–6, intended to be used with a parent's or guardian's involvement. Signing in with a Google account gives full access right away — there's nothing to request or wait for.

Your sign-in is personal — please don't share it. Each child should sign in with their own account.

02 Always free

Gnaana-Kosha is completely free to use. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no “premium” tier, and nothing to buy — now or later.

03 Privacy & your data

We keep data to the bare minimum needed to run a private, personal learning space:

  • Your sign-in email is used only to grant and manage access to your account.
  • Your learning progress and badges are stored on your own device, in your browser — not on a central server that profiles your child.
  • There is no third-party advertising and no behavioural tracking. Your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

A parent or guardian can ask to remove a child's access or data at any time — just contact us using the details below.

04 About the content

The learning material is independently prepared and based on public-domain and widely-verified facts, then put through multiple fact-checking passes. We take accuracy seriously — especially for children.

Even so, it is provided “as is” for learning and enrichment, and is not a replacement for a school curriculum, exam preparation, or professional advice. If you ever spot a mistake, please let us know so we can fix it.

05 Being a good guest

Please use Gnaana-Kosha kindly and as intended — for a child's personal learning. Don't try to disrupt, misuse, overload, or gain unauthorised access to the service.

06 Availability & changes

Gnaana-Kosha is offered as a free, best-effort service and may change, pause, or be updated at any time; we can't guarantee it will always be available or error-free. These conditions may be updated from time to time, and continued use means you accept the current version.

07 Questions

For anything at all — access, a correction, or a data request — email us at support.gnaanakosha@gmail.com.
